HOW TO PLAY

Choose a champion card, then tap your side of the arena to deploy. Fighters move and attack automatically. Protect your core and destroy the enemy core before three minutes run out.

Energy regenerates over time. Each champion costs a different amount. Use the ultimate to turn a close fight around.

Local Friend Battle is pass-and-play on the same device: Blue deploys, then Red deploys. Adaptive AI adjusts its counters based on your most-used champion.
