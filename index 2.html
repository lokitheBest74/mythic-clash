How to play

Quick rules
  1. Choose one card from your hand to summon each turn. Summoning costs energy.
  2. Tap one of your active characters, then tap an enemy to choose a target.
  3. Use Attack for damage or Ability for that character's special move. Each character can act once per turn.
  4. Tap End Turn to let the AI attack. Energy recovers by 2, up to 10.
  5. Defeat every enemy on the field to win. You can retrieve extra cards from the top-right button.
